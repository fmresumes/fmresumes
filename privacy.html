Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 13, 2026. This policy explains how Brenvo Core collects, uses, stores, and protects information connected with our recruitment, staffing, HR, and career services.

1. Information we collect

We may collect your name, contact details, company information, job title, resume/CV content, career history, education, certifications, LinkedIn profile details, service preferences, messages, and any information you choose to provide during consultations or service delivery.

2. How we use information

We use information to respond to inquiries, provide recruitment and staffing services, evaluate candidate suitability, prepare resumes, cover letters, and LinkedIn content, deliver HR or workforce consulting, manage client relationships, improve our services, and meet legal or administrative obligations.

3. Recruitment and staffing data

Candidate information may be reviewed for role fit and, with appropriate consent or legitimate business purpose, shared with relevant clients, hiring teams, or service providers involved in the recruitment process. We do not sell candidate personal data.

4. Career services data

Career documents and professional histories are used to prepare requested services such as resume writing, LinkedIn optimization, cover letters, and career counseling. We treat career materials as confidential client information.

5. Legal bases and consent

Depending on your location, we may rely on consent, contract performance, legitimate interests, legal obligations, or another lawful basis to process personal information. You may withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

6. Service providers

We may use trusted providers for hosting, email, form handling, document management, analytics, or operational support. Providers are expected to protect information and use it only for authorized purposes.

7. Data security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect information. No internet transmission or storage method is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Retention

We keep information only as long as needed for service delivery, client records, recruitment activity, legal obligations, dispute resolution, and legitimate business purposes. You may request deletion subject to applicable exceptions.

9. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, or withdrawal of consent. To make a request, contact hello@brenvocore.com.

10. International transfers

Brenvo Core serves international clients and professionals. Information may be processed in countries other than your own, with safeguards used where required by applicable law.

11. Cookies and analytics

Our website may use basic cookies or analytics to understand performance and improve user experience. You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email hello@brenvocore.com.
